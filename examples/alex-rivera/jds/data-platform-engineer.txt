Data Platform Engineer
Cobalt Meadow Analytics (an invented company, written for this example)

What you will do
- Build batch data pipelines in Python and SQL that load, test and publish analytics tables.
- Model data with dbt and schedule it with Airflow.
- Keep the warehouse fast and the data trustworthy: add tests, track freshness and fix failures.
- Work with analysts to replace manual reports with automated ones.

What we look for
- Experience with Python, SQL and a columnar warehouse such as Snowflake or DuckDB.
- Experience with dbt and a workflow scheduler.
- Care about data quality and the ability to explain a pipeline to non-engineers.

Nice to have
- Parquet, Docker and CI experience.
