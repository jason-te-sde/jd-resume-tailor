Backend Engineer, Shipment Tracking
Harbor & Pine Logistics (an invented company, written for this example)

About the role
Harbor & Pine moves freight for regional retailers, and the tracking API is the part our customers see. We are looking for a backend engineer to keep it fast and correct.

What you will do
- Build and operate Go services behind the shipment-tracking API, backed by PostgreSQL and Kafka.
- Find and fix latency problems, using load tests and dashboards to show the change.
- Design event-driven pipelines that keep tracking data consistent across services.
- Carry the pager for the services you own and improve their alerts and runbooks.

What we look for
- Experience writing production services in Go or a similar language.
- Solid SQL and an understanding of indexing and caching.
- Comfort with Docker, CI pipelines and infrastructure as code.

Nice to have
- Experience with Redis, gRPC or Terraform.
