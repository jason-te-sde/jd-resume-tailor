Distributed Systems Engineer
Quillfeather Systems (an invented company, written for this example)

What you will do
- Implement and test consensus, replication and storage components in Rust or Go.
- Write simulators and property tests that find failures before production does.
- Benchmark systems under load, find the bottleneck and document the evidence.
- Design recovery paths so that a node crash does not lose acknowledged writes.

What we look for
- Strong systems programming in Rust, Go or C++.
- Understanding of consensus protocols, write-ahead logs and crash recovery.
- A habit of measuring: you report medians, percentiles and the conditions of each run.

Nice to have
- Experience with Redis internals or embedded key-value stores.
