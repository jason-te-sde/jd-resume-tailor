Engineering Manager, Data Services
Sorrel Ridge Software (an invented company, written for this evaluation)

About the role
Sorrel Ridge sells inventory software to small manufacturers. The data services team of seven engineers owns our integrations and reporting. We are looking for a manager who can grow the team and keep delivery steady.

What you will do
- Manage and develop a team of seven engineers: hiring, onboarding, one-on-ones, feedback and promotions.
- Own the team's roadmap and delivery with product management, and report progress to the director.
- Set the team's engineering practices: code review, on-call, incident follow-up and planning.
- Handle performance concerns with care and clarity.
- Work with customers' technical staff on integration problems.
- Stay close enough to the code to review designs and ask good questions.

What we look for
- Three or more years managing engineers and five or more years as an engineer.
- A track record of building a team and keeping it healthy through change.
- Experience with data integrations, APIs and relational databases.
- Strong communication with executives and customers.

Nice to have
- Experience managing a team through a platform migration.
- Experience with budgeting and vendor selection.
