Storage Engine Engineer
Granite Reach Data (an invented company, written for this evaluation)

About the role
Granite Reach builds a time-series database for industrial telemetry. The storage engine decides how fast we write, how quickly we recover and how much disk we use, and we are hiring a second engineer to work on it.

What you will do
- Improve the write path of our log-structured storage engine: memtables, write-ahead logging, flushing and compaction.
- Make crash recovery fast and correct, and prove it with fault-injection tests.
- Benchmark rigorously and explain performance changes, with profiles instead of guesses.
- Design the on-disk format and its upgrade path.
- Contribute to replication between nodes and the consistency guarantees we document.
- Review code from other engineers with an eye for concurrency and durability bugs.

What we look for
- Experience writing systems code in Go, Rust or C++.
- Understanding of LSM trees, B-trees, fsync semantics and page caches.
- Experience with concurrency, profiling and benchmarking.
- Experience with distributed consensus protocols such as Raft or Paxos.

Nice to have
- Experience with columnar formats or vectorised execution.
- Contributions to an open-source database.
