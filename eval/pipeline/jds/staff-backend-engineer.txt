Staff Backend Engineer, Platform Architecture
Ironbark Payments (an invented company, written for this evaluation)

About the role
Ironbark processes card and bank payments for subscription businesses. We have grown from one service to forty, and we need a staff engineer to set technical direction across teams, not only to write code.

What you will do
- Set the architecture for our payment-processing platform and drive decisions across eight teams.
- Lead multi-quarter migrations from a monolith to services, including data ownership and rollout plans.
- Define reliability and consistency standards for money movement: idempotency, reconciliation and exactly-once effects.
- Write and review design documents, and unblock senior engineers who disagree.
- Mentor and grow senior engineers, and partner with directors on roadmap and hiring.
- Represent engineering in conversations with auditors and enterprise customers.

What we look for
- Ten or more years of backend engineering, with five or more years at staff level or equivalent scope.
- A record of leading technical work across several teams and delivering it in production.
- Deep experience with distributed transactions, queues and relational databases at scale.
- Experience with PCI compliance and regulated environments.
- Experience with Java or Kotlin in a large codebase.

Nice to have
- Experience designing event sourcing or ledger systems.
- Experience running an on-call rotation for a payments system.
