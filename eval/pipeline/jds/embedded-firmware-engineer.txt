Embedded Firmware Engineer
Pelican Rock Devices (an invented company, written for this evaluation)

About the role
Pelican Rock designs battery-powered soil sensors that run for three years on a cell. The firmware is small, careful and has to work without anyone ever touching the device. We are looking for an engineer to join the three-person firmware team.

What you will do
- Write firmware in C for ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers and bring up new boards.
- Implement low-power sleep states, interrupts and drivers for I2C, SPI and UART sensors.
- Build the over-the-air update path and the bootloader, with rollback when an update fails.
- Work with hardware engineers to debug with oscilloscopes, logic analysers and JTAG.
- Write unit tests and hardware-in-the-loop tests for the firmware.
- Profile power draw and bring it down milliamp by milliamp.

What we look for
- Three or more years of embedded C or C++ on microcontrollers.
- Experience with an RTOS such as FreeRTOS or Zephyr.
- Comfort reading schematics and datasheets.
- Experience with BLE or LoRa radio stacks.

Nice to have
- Experience with Rust on embedded targets.
- Experience with functional safety or medical device standards.
