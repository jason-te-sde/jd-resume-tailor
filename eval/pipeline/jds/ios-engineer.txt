iOS Engineer
Tamarind Fitness (an invented company, written for this evaluation)

About the role
Tamarind builds a workout and recovery app for runners and cyclists. Our iOS app is where users spend their day, and we are hiring an engineer to make it faster, more reliable and a pleasure to use.

What you will do
- Build new features in Swift and SwiftUI for our iPhone and Apple Watch apps.
- Integrate HealthKit, Core Location and Bluetooth sensors, and handle background execution correctly.
- Improve launch time, memory use and battery impact using Instruments.
- Work with designers on animations and accessibility with VoiceOver and Dynamic Type.
- Write unit and UI tests and keep the release pipeline healthy with Xcode Cloud.
- Ship every two weeks through TestFlight and the App Store.

What we look for
- Three or more years building iOS apps in Swift, with at least one app in the App Store.
- Experience with SwiftUI, Combine or Swift concurrency, and Core Data.
- An eye for detail on animation and interaction.

Nice to have
- Experience with Apple Watch or widgets.
- Some Kotlin or Android experience.
