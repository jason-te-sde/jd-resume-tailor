Site Reliability Engineer
Basalt Wharf Cloud (an invented company, written for this evaluation)

About the role
Basalt Wharf runs a hosted scheduling platform for clinics, and it has to be up when a front desk opens at 7 a.m. We are growing the reliability team and are looking for an engineer who is happy to be paged and happier to make the page unnecessary.

What you will do
- Own the availability targets for our core services: define service level objectives, track error budgets and decide with product teams when to slow down.
- Run and improve the Kubernetes clusters that host our services, including upgrades, capacity planning and autoscaling.
- Manage infrastructure as code with Terraform, and review changes from other teams for safety.
- Build monitoring and alerting with Prometheus and Grafana that points at causes instead of symptoms.
- Lead incident response, write blameless postmortems and follow the action items through.
- Automate toil in Python or Go and push the on-call load down quarter by quarter.

What we look for
- Three or more years running production services on a major cloud, preferably AWS.
- Hands-on experience with Kubernetes, Linux and networking fundamentals.
- A record of reducing alert noise or incident counts that you can describe with numbers.
- Clear written communication: runbooks, postmortems and design notes.

Nice to have
- Experience with service meshes, chaos testing or progressive delivery.
- Familiarity with PostgreSQL operations and backup and restore drills.
